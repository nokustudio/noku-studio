Our products

  • Our furniture is made by hand from solid wood: teak from vendors in South India, certified kiln-dried white ash imported from abroad, and, for some pieces, teak reclaimed from old buildings in South India. Some bespoke pieces, and the inner supports of our teak beds, are made from honne. Grain, colour, knots and small marks vary from piece to piece and from the photographs. That variation is part of the material, not a defect.
  • Photographs and on-screen colours are a guide only. Screens differ, and finishes look different under different light.
  • Dimensions may vary slightly because each piece is made by hand.
  • Some pieces are listed for enquiry only. Pieces made to your specifications (“bespoke”) are agreed by quote. See Bespoke orders below.

Orders

Your order is confirmed when you receive our order confirmation email. We may decline or cancel an order, for example if an item is unavailable or we can’t deliver to your address. If we do, we refund you in full.

Bespoke orders

Our collection is a starting point. We can resize a piece, change its wood, finish or upholstery, or make something new from your reference, drawing or idea. Tell us what you have in mind and we’ll come back with what’s possible, the specifications and a quote. Changes to size or materials change the price, and the quote will show this.

Your quote

Each bespoke order has a written quote. It sets out the specifications, price, payment schedule, estimated timeline, and how delivery and installation are charged. If anything in the quote differs from these terms, the quote applies.

Payment

  • 100% advance to confirm your order. We don’t prepare your order confirmation or start production until it is paid.
  • Delivery within Bangalore is charged separately and quoted with your order. Outside Bangalore, you arrange transport from our studio (see Shipping & Delivery).

Order confirmation

  • Once we receive your advance, we finalise the wood, finish and any upholstery with you. You can choose materials at our Bangalore studio, or we can send samples or walk you through them on a video call.
  • We then send you an order confirmation document showing each piece with its size and seat height, and the materials, upholstery and finish options chosen. Detailed technical drawings are not shared.
  • Production starts only after you confirm this document in writing. A reply by email or WhatsApp counts as written confirmation.
  • Once production starts, sizes and materials can’t be changed. Please check every detail carefully before you confirm.
  • Samples show the material and finish. Each piece of wood is different, so the finished piece will vary slightly from the sample.

Design decisions

All final design choices, hardware and consumables are decided by Noku Studio. You are welcome to make suggestions, but the final call is ours.

Timelines

Bespoke pieces take a minimum of 8 weeks from the date you confirm your order. The exact time depends on how complex the design and production are, and your quote will give an estimate. It can change with material availability, especially for reclaimed teak and fabrics, and with how complex the piece is. If your timeline changes, we’ll tell you.

Size and access

Before you confirm your order, please check that the piece will fit through doors, stairs, lifts and corridors at the delivery address. We’re happy to help: send us measurements or photos and we’ll advise you, and we can suggest solutions such as a piece that comes apart. If a confirmed piece can’t be delivered into the space because of access, we can’t take it back or remake it free of charge, and any extra handling or redelivery is charged at actual cost.

Cancelling a bespoke order

Once you’ve confirmed your order, the advance is non-refundable, because your piece is made only for you.

Delivery is covered by our Shipping & Delivery policy. We don’t accept returns or exchanges on any piece. Damaged or defective pieces are replaced. Cancellations, refunds and replacements are covered by our Returns, Refunds & Cancellations policy.

Warranty

Our furniture is covered against manufacturing defects under normal household use, from the date of delivery or collection:

  • Wood, joinery and structure: 5 years. This covers defects in the wood or construction, joints loosening or separating, and frame failure.
  • Finish and upholstery: 1 year. This covers the finish peeling or bubbling, seams splitting, and foam, webbing or springs failing early.

If a covered defect appears, we will repair the piece or, if it can’t be repaired, replace it.

What the warranty doesn’t cover

The warranty covers manufacturing defects only. It does not cover the following.

  • Natural character of wood: variation in grain, colour, knots, mineral streaks and texture between pieces, or between a piece and a sample or photograph. It also excludes colour that deepens or lightens with age and light, and old nail holes, marks and patina on reclaimed teak.
  • Wood movement from the environment: small surface cracks (checks), slight warping, gaps, expansion or shrinkage caused by humidity or temperature. This includes pieces placed near air conditioners, heaters, radiators, damp walls or in direct sunlight.
  • Water and moisture damage: water rings, swelling, mould or finish damage from spills, wet cleaning, plants, leaks or damp floors.
  • Pests: termite, borer or other pest damage that starts from the environment the piece is kept in after delivery.
  • Normal wear and tear: scratches, dents, chips, stains, burns, rubbing marks, and fading or discolouration from sunlight.
  • Fabric and leather wear: pilling, fading, creasing, stretching, colour transfer from clothing (such as denim), and cushions softening or settling with use.
  • Accidents, misuse and overloading: dropping, standing or jumping on furniture, sitting on arms or backs, leaning back on chair legs, loads beyond normal household use, and damage from pets or children.
  • Improper care: harsh chemicals, polishes or solvents, abrasive cleaners, or not following our care guide.
  • Outdoor or commercial use: using indoor furniture outdoors or in semi-open spaces, or in commercial settings such as cafés, offices, hotels or rentals, unless we’ve agreed to this in writing.
  • Moving and transport: damage during transport arranged by you, after handover at our studio, or when the piece is moved, dismantled or relocated after delivery.
  • Changes by others: repairs, alterations, refinishing, reupholstery or assembly by anyone other than Noku.
  • Customer-supplied materials: fabric, leather, hardware or other materials you provide, and defects caused by them.
  • Small differences: slight variation in size, and differences from samples, photographs or on-screen colours.
  • Hardware and consumable parts: normal wear of glides, felt pads and similar parts.
  • Other losses: indirect costs, such as loss of use, or the cost of moving, storing or transporting the piece for inspection or repair outside Bangalore.

The warranty covers the original purchaser only. It can’t be transferred if the piece is sold or given away. If you’re not sure whether something is covered, email us photos at hello@nokustudio.in and we’ll take a look. These exclusions don’t affect your rights under Indian consumer law.
