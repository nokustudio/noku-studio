Our products

  • Our furniture is made by hand from solid, reclaimed and locally sourced wood. Grain, colour, knots and small marks vary from piece to piece and from the photographs. That variation is part of the material, not a defect.

Orders

Your order is confirmed when you receive our order confirmation email. We may decline or cancel an order, for example if an item is unavailable or we can’t deliver to your address. If we do, we refund you in full.

Bespoke orders

Our collection is a starting point. We can resize a piece, change its wood, finish or upholstery, or make something new from your reference, drawing or idea. Tell us what you have in mind and we’ll come back with what’s possible, the specifications and a quote. Changes to size or materials change the price, and the quote will show this.

Your quote

Each bespoke order has a written quote. It sets out the specifications, price, payment schedule, estimated timeline, and how delivery and installation are charged. If anything in the quote differs from these terms, the quote applies.

Payment

  • An advance, at the amount stated in your quote, confirms your order. We don’t start drawings or production until it is paid.
  • The balance is due before dispatch, as stated in your quote. We dispatch your piece once it is paid in full.
  • Unless your quote includes them, transport, handling and installation are charged at actual cost and paid on delivery. Your quote will give an estimate.

Drawings and approval

  • Once we receive your advance, we prepare a detailed drawing and finalise the wood, finish and any upholstery with you. You can choose materials at our Bangalore studio, or we can send samples or walk you through them on a video call.
  • Production starts only after you approve the final drawing and specifications in writing. A reply by email or WhatsApp counts as written approval.
  • Once production starts, the design, dimensions and materials can’t be changed. Please check every detail carefully before you approve.
  • Samples show the material and finish. Each piece of wood is different, so the finished piece will vary slightly from the sample.

Timelines

The timeline in your quote starts from the date you approve the design. It is an estimate. It can change with material availability, especially for reclaimed wood and fabrics, and with how complex the piece is. If your timeline changes, we’ll tell you.

Size and access

Before you approve the design, please check that the piece will fit through doors, stairs, lifts and corridors at the delivery address. We’re happy to help: send us measurements or photos and we’ll advise you, and we can suggest solutions such as a piece that comes apart. If an approved piece can’t be delivered into the space because of access, we can’t take it back or remake it free of charge, and any extra handling or redelivery is charged at actual cost.

Cancelling a bespoke order

If you cancel before approving the final drawing, we refund your advance, less any design charges stated in your quote. Once you’ve approved the design, the advance is non-refundable, because your piece is made only for you.

Delivery, returns and cancellations

Delivery is covered by our Shipping & Delivery policy. Cancellations, returns and refunds are covered by our Returns, Refunds & Cancellations policy.
