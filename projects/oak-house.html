Spaces

Brief

The focus of this project was on the bedrooms, where we wanted to bring in an old-school richness through the interiors and furniture. Each bedroom had a distinct color palette that reflected the user’s aesthetics. The furniture was designed to blend seamlessly with the overall space, working in harmony with other elements rather than standing out individually. So, although the furniture, in its essence, was to be more aligned with function than form, the magic happened when everything came together.

  1. 01The beds

    The three beds were designed around a similar base structure with changes coming in the upholstered headboards and storage underneath.

  2. 02Daughter's bedroom

    The daughter's bedroom had a pastel and floral theme according to the client's preferences. We chose a clean headboard with a minimal wrap around the bed to make it stand out from the panel behind.

  3. 03Workspace & storage

    Teak wood textures with simple custom details were integrated throughout the workspace and side storage units, achieving a unified theme.
