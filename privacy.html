Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Noku Studio (“Noku”, “we”, “us”) collects through nokustudio.in, why, and what you can do about it. Noku Studio is a registered business in Bangalore, Karnataka.

What we collect

  • Enquiries. When you use our contact form or a product enquiry form, we collect your name, email address, phone number (if you give it) and your message.
  • Orders. When you check out, Shopify collects your name, email, phone, billing and delivery address on our behalf. Card, UPI and bank details are handled directly by Shopify and its payment partners. We never see or store them.
  • Usage data. We use Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to count page visits and measure page speed. These tools don’t use cookies and don’t identify you personally.
  • Your cart. Your cart is saved in your own browser (local storage) so it survives a page reload. It stays on your device.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare quotes for bespoke work.
  • To process, make, deliver and support your order, including invoices and GST records.
  • To understand which pages are useful and keep the site fast.

We don’t sell your data, and we don’t send marketing messages unless you’ve asked for them.

Who we share it with

Only the services needed to run the shop:

  • Shopify for checkout, payments and order records.
  • Zoho Forms to receive enquiry forms.
  • Vercel to host the website and provide anonymous analytics.
  • Courier and logistics partners, who receive your name, phone number and delivery address so they can deliver your order.

We may also share data where the law requires it, for example with tax authorities or in response to a lawful order. Some of these providers store data outside India and are bound by their own privacy and security commitments.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as the conversation is active and for up to two years afterwards. We keep order and invoice records for eight years, as Indian tax law requires.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it, unless we must keep it by law (for example, invoices);
  • withdraw any consent you’ve given;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf.

Write to hello@nokustudio.in and we’ll respond within 30 days.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS and payments are processed by PCI-DSS compliant providers. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

This site is not meant for anyone under 18. We don’t knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
