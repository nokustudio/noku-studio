Materiality

The Soul of the Object

We believe a piece of furniture is only as honest as the materials from which it is born. At Noku Studio, we do not mask the organic. We celebrate it.

Explore our materials→

01Reclaimed Teak

Rescued from decades-old structures in Southern India—seasoned naturally by sun and monsoon, carrying a warm history and unparalleled stability.

02Natural Rattan Cane

Each strand is meticulously hand-woven by local master artisans, marrying breathing mid-century forms with India's living heritage.

03Certified Hides

Left aniline-finished, preserving the natural grain, scars, and creases, designed to acquire a unique, rich patina with every touch.

04Woven & Printed Fabrics

Hard-wearing, easy-care upholstery weaves, cottons hand-woven on Kerala looms, and cloth hand-block-printed by artisans—no two prints quite alike.
