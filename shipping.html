Shipping & Delivery

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Every piece is packed by hand in reusable blankets, corrugated cardboard and biodegradable bindings, with no single-use plastic. Here’s how we get it to you.

Where we deliver

We deliver across India. For some remote locations we may need extra time or to agree a delivery plan with you before dispatch. For deliveries outside India, email hello@nokustudio.in for a quote.

How long it takes

  • Ready-to-ship pieces: dispatched within [7–10] business days of your order.
  • Made-to-order pieces: made and dispatched within [4–8 weeks]. Your order confirmation shows the estimate for your piece.
  • Bespoke pieces: the timeline in your quote applies, counted from the date you approve the design. Delivery and installation charges are as stated in your quote.
  • Transit: once dispatched, delivery usually takes 3–7 business days within Bangalore and nearby cities, and 7–14 business days elsewhere in India.

These are estimates. If your order will be late, we’ll tell you as soon as we know.

Charges

Delivery charges depend on the size of the piece and your location, and are shown at checkout before you pay. [Add any free-delivery rule, e.g. free within Bangalore]

Tracking and delivery day

  • We’ll email or call you with tracking details once your order is dispatched.
  • Large pieces are delivered by our logistics partner, who will call you to schedule the delivery. Please make sure someone is available to receive it.
  • Delivery is to the ground floor or the building entrance unless we’ve agreed otherwise. Please check that the piece will fit through doors, stairs, lifts and corridors before you order. Send us measurements or photos if you’re unsure and we’ll advise. We can’t accept returns because a piece doesn’t fit, and extra handling or redelivery is charged at actual cost.
  • If a delivery fails because no one was available or the address was wrong, redelivery may cost extra.

Assembly

Most pieces arrive fully assembled. If a piece needs assembly, it comes with instructions and any tools needed. In Bangalore we can arrange assembly on request.

Damage in transit

Please check your piece on delivery and tell us about any damage within 48 hours. See our Returns, Refunds & Cancellations policy for how we fix it.
