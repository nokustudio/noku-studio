Returns, Refunds & Cancellations

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Our furniture is made carefully and in small numbers, and many pieces are made after you order. This page explains what you can cancel or return, and what happens if something arrives damaged.

Cancellations

  • Ready-to-ship pieces: you can cancel for a full refund any time before the order is dispatched.
  • Made-to-order pieces: you can cancel for a full refund within 48 hours of placing the order. After that, work on your piece has usually started, and we will refund the amount paid minus [25%] of the order value to cover materials and labour already used.
  • Bespoke pieces (custom size, wood, finish or design): you can cancel for a refund of your advance, less any design charges in your quote, until you approve the final drawing. After approval the advance is non-refundable. See Bespoke orders.

To cancel, email hello@nokustudio.in with your order number.

Returns

You can return ready-to-ship pieces within 7 days of delivery if they are unused, undamaged and in their original packaging. Contact us first so we can arrange collection. Return shipping is charged at cost and deducted from your refund.

We can’t accept returns on made-to-order or bespoke pieces, because they are made specifically for you. We will still fix any defect or transit damage, as described below.

Damaged or defective items

  • Please check your piece when it’s delivered. If you can see damage to the packaging or the furniture, note it on the delivery receipt.
  • Tell us within 48 hours of delivery, and send photos of the damage and the packaging to hello@nokustudio.in.
  • We’ll repair the piece, replace it, or refund you in full, whichever suits the damage best. You pay nothing for this.

Natural variations in grain, colour or knots, and small changes from humidity, are not defects (see our Terms & Conditions).

Refunds

  • Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 7–10 business days. Your bank may take a few more days to show the credit.
  • For returns, the refund is issued once the piece reaches our workshop and passes inspection.
  • We’ll email you when the refund has been issued.
